Menu bar app for Mac · Product design jobs, Australia

Product design jobs in Australia, in one place.

Job boards, company career pages and freelance sites each show you a slice. OHMAA gathers the roles into one list in your Mac menu bar, newest first, so you can check once and get on with your day.

Search

Find the role you want.

Type a role, company or city. OHMAA searches titles, companies, locations, work arrangement, salary and stage.

Each extra word narrows the list. “sydney hybrid” shows only roles that match both.

Press Return to open the top match on its original listing. ⌘F jumps to search and Esc clears it.

One list

Stop checking every job site.

Open OHMAA and the roles are already there, gathered from job boards, company career pages and freelance sites into a single list.

Newest first

Today’s roles on top.

Roles are sorted by the day they were found, so what turned up this morning is at the top. Roles you have applied to, and closed ones, stay hidden.

Easy to scan

Four roles. One glance.

Each row shows the company logo, the role, the location and whether it is remote, hybrid or onsite. One click opens the rest of the list.

Made for macOS

Looks right in light and dark.

On macOS 26 the panel uses Liquid Glass and follows your appearance setting. With Reduce Transparency on, it switches to a solid background. Earlier macOS versions get the standard frosted look.

Install

Installed in a minute.

  1. Open the disk image

    Double-click OHMAA.dmg after it downloads.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Drop OHMAA onto the Applications folder in the window that opens.

  3. Open it the first time

    Right-click OHMAA in Applications and choose Open. macOS asks once, because the app is not notarized. Then look for the briefcase in your menu bar.
